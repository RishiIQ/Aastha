Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 — Safeguarding your data with utmost reverence.

1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Aastha Pooja Store, we treat your personal information with the same sacred respect and confidentiality as our devotional offerings. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, secure, and utilize your data when you interact with our website and multi-page store suite.

2. Information We Collect

To fulfill festival kit orders, manage subscriptions, and provide responsive customer concierge support, we collect specific categories of data:

  • Identity & Profile Data: Full name, username, and account preferences.
  • Contact & Delivery Data: 10-digit telephone number, verified email address, and complete physical shipping address.
  • Secure Financial Data: Encrypted payment tokens, UPI IDs (e.g., username@okaxis), and truncated card information processed safely through certified third-party gateway providers.
  • Telemetry & Session Data: Browser type, dark mode preferences, and cart synchronization states via local storage.

3. How Your Data is Utilized

Your information is exclusively used for operational and devotional service excellence:

  • Processing and dispatching consecrated brass artifacts, ghee wicks, and festival kits before auspicious tithis.
  • Providing real-time order tracking notifications via SMS, WhatsApp, and email.
  • Sending monthly panchang calendar updates and member-only festival discounts (if subscribed).
  • Maintaining platform security and complying with statutory financial record-keeping laws.

4. Data Encryption & Security Protocols

We implement enterprise-grade security architecture, including SSL encryption, restricted administrative access, and strict client-side validation for all checkout and payment forms. Your raw payment credentials are never stored on our local servers.

5. Questions & Data Inquiries

If you wish to review, update, or request the deletion of your personal data from our systems, please reach out to our privacy compliance desk at privacy@aasthapooja.com or contact us directly.
